A FELINE FIELD GUIDE

Life with
cats.

From a slow blink across the room to a midnight burst of play, get to know the animal who shares your home.

Read the signals

Speaking cat

Watch the ears, tail and posture together. The same gesture can mean different things during play and during a surprise encounter.

The slow blink

A relaxed cat may soften their eyes and close them slowly. Try a gentle blink back, then give them time to approach.

The upright tail

A cat walking toward you with a raised tail and relaxed ears may be greeting you. Offer a still hand and let them choose whether to rub against it.

The tail flick

A sharply flicking tail during petting can signal rising irritation. Pause and watch whether your cat leans back in or walks away.

Everyday rituals

Make room to scratch

Place a sturdy scratching post near a favorite sleeping spot. Offer a horizontal pad too, and see which surface your cat returns to.

Let the hunt end

Move a wand toy like small prey, pausing behind a chair leg. Let your cat catch it at the end and put strings away after play.

Keep a quiet corner

A cardboard box or covered bed gives your cat somewhere to retreat. Leave the exit clear so they can come and go on their own.
